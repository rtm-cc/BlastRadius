Curate confirmed data breaches and security incidents at named organizations: who was breached, what data was exposed, how many people are affected, how the attackers got in, and what the organization is doing about it. Include breach disclosures, regulatory breach notifications, leaked databases, account takeovers at scale, and new breaches added to Have I Been Pwned. Exclude unverified claims posted by attackers with no corroboration, breach anniversaries and retrospectives, statistics reports, and vendor marketing. Leave the vulnerability that was exploited to the Vulnerabilities column and the ransomware group's campaign to the Active Threats column, unless the breach itself is the news. Judge relevance by the scale and sensitivity of the data and how many people need to act, such as resetting passwords or watching for fraud.
