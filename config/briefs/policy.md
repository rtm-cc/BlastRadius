Curate cybersecurity policy and enforcement: new laws and regulations with deadlines (such as NIS2, the Cyber Resilience Act, DORA and the AI Act), regulator fines and rulings, data protection decisions, law enforcement takedowns, arrests, indictments, sanctions, and government advisories that change what organizations must do. Exclude opinion pieces, consultations with no decision, political commentary, and conference talks. Judge relevance by whether it changes an obligation, sets a precedent, or removes a real threat actor or infrastructure.
