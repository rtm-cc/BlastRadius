Curate active threats: ransomware attacks and the groups behind them, malware campaigns and new malware families, botnets, nation-state operations and espionage, and new attacker techniques seen in the wild, with who is targeted and how. Prefer reporting with evidence from incident responders and threat researchers. Exclude generic threat landscape reports and statistics, opinion pieces, unsubstantiated attribution, and product announcements. Leave the breached organization's disclosure to the Breaches column, phishing lures and scams aimed at individuals to the Fraud & Phishing column, and arrests and takedowns to the Policy & Enforcement column. Judge relevance by how active and widespread the threat is and whether defenders can do something about it today.
