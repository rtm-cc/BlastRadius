Curate vulnerabilities and patches that someone should act on: zero-days, vulnerabilities exploited in the wild, additions to CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, critical flaws in widely used software, devices and cloud services, emergency and out-of-band patches, and Patch Tuesday highlights. Name the affected product, the CVE where there is one, whether it is being exploited, and whether a fix exists. Exclude bare CVE identifiers with no context, low-severity issues in niche products, theoretical research with no realistic attack path, and vendor marketing. Judge relevance by exploitation first, then by how widely the affected software is deployed: an exploited flaw in a popular product always comes before an unexploited one.
